Frequently Asked Questions

If your question isn't answered here, it's a good reason to reach out directly.

A personal concierge handles the planning, coordination, and follow-through on tasks in your life — from travel and dining to home management and events — so you don't have to manage the details yourself.

Our home base is the Connecticut shoreline and the Tri-State area, with standing relationships across Rhode Island, the Hudson Valley, the Berkshires, and Manhattan. Membership is not limited to one address.

Membership begins with an application, not a checkout. We review every application personally and follow up with a short conversation to make sure it's a good fit on both sides before any payment is discussed.

Discretion is central to how we operate. Member information is never shared, sold, or discussed outside of what's needed to fulfill a specific request, and only with partners you've approved.

Response times depend on your membership tier — from standard next-business-day handling up to 24-hour availability for Estate members. Non-members can also submit a one-off request through Request Concierge.

Pricing depends on tier and scope, and is set during your application conversation rather than published as a flat rate — every household's needs are different.
